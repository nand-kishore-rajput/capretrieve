# Meal Prep

#nutrition #mealprep

## How I do it

- **When:** Sunday, 15:00 to 17:30. Podcast on, kitchen gets messy
- **What:** 5 lunches for the work week (Mon to Fri), plus 2 to 3 dinners frozen as backup for late study evenings
- **Containers:** 10 glass containers, 1 litre each, bought in January for 38 EUR. The plastic ones stained orange from curry
- **Shopping:** Saturday morning at the discount supermarket, plus the Turkish grocer for vegetables, spices and bulgur (much cheaper there)
- **Budget:** around 35 to 40 EUR a week for prepped food. Groceries are a fixed line in my [[Monthly Budget]] at 300 EUR a month, which covers prep plus breakfast, weekend cooking and snacks

Why I started (February 2025): I was spending about 9 to 11 EUR on lunch at the canteen or the bakery on home office days, and eating chocolate at 22:00 because there was nothing proper in the fridge. Meal prep fixed both. It also made the [[Intermittent Fasting]] experiment work, because I had a real meal ready the second my eating window opened.

## Rotation (spring and summer 2025)

Four recipes I rotate. Macros per portion, calculated with a food tracking app using the actual package labels. Roughly right, not exact.

### 1. Chicken, rice and roasted vegetables
- 750 g chicken breast, 300 g white rice (dry), 2 bell peppers, 2 zucchini, 1 red onion, olive oil, paprika, garlic
- Oven at 200 degrees, 25 minutes for everything on two trays
- **Per portion (x5):** about 560 kcal, 48 g protein, 62 g carbs, 12 g fat
- Cost: about 14 EUR for 5 portions

### 2. Red lentil curry
- 400 g red lentils, 1 can coconut milk (light), 1 can chopped tomatoes, 2 onions, ginger, garlic, curry paste, 200 g spinach
- One pot, 30 minutes
- **Per portion (x5):** about 480 kcal, 22 g protein, 58 g carbs, 16 g fat
- Cost: about 7 EUR for 5 portions. Cheapest and the one I like most

### 3. Turkey chili
- 500 g turkey mince, 2 cans kidney beans, 1 can corn, 2 cans tomatoes, 2 peppers, chili, cumin, smoked paprika
- **Per portion (x5):** about 520 kcal, 42 g protein, 50 g carbs, 13 g fat
- Cost: about 12 EUR. Freezes perfectly

### 4. Pasta with tuna, olives and tomato
- 400 g whole wheat pasta, 3 cans tuna, olives, capers, cherry tomatoes, lemon
- **Per portion (x5):** about 590 kcal, 38 g protein, 72 g carbs, 14 g fat
- Cost: about 11 EUR. Good cold, which matters on office days without a microwave free at 12:00

Daily targets (summer 2025): roughly 2,300 kcal and 130 g protein on run days, 2,000 kcal on rest days. Breakfast was skipped during fasting, otherwise skyr with oats and berries.

## Changes after the October blood test

After the HbA1c result (7.2%, details in my health notes), the doctor asked me to cut down on refined carbs and eat more vegetables, legumes and protein. So from 2025-10-20 the rotation changes:

- **White rice out**, replaced with bulgur, lentils or extra roasted vegetables
- **Whole wheat pasta** stays but only once every 2 weeks, half the portion, more vegetables in the sauce
- **Every lunch** must be at least half vegetables by volume
- **Protein** at least 35 g per lunch
- **No juice** in the shopping basket. I was buying a litre of orange juice a week and telling myself it was healthy

### New recipe: bulgur bowl with chickpeas and feta
- 250 g bulgur (dry), 2 cans chickpeas, 1 cucumber, 300 g cherry tomatoes, 200 g feta, parsley, lemon, olive oil
- **Per portion (x5):** about 510 kcal, 22 g protein, 55 g carbs, 20 g fat
- Protein a bit low, so I add a boiled egg or 100 g of chicken. With chicken: about 620 kcal, 45 g protein
- Cost: about 13 EUR

### New recipe: chicken and vegetable tray, no rice
- 800 g chicken thigh (cheaper and juicier than breast), broccoli, cauliflower, carrots, red onion, chickpeas for some carbs
- **Per portion (x5):** about 470 kcal, 44 g protein, 28 g carbs, 18 g fat
- Cost: about 15 EUR

### Lentil curry, adjusted
- Same as before, more spinach, served with cauliflower rice on 2 of the 5 days instead of nothing. Added 200 g of tofu for protein
- **Per portion:** about 510 kcal, 30 g protein, 50 g carbs, 18 g fat

## Tips that actually help

- Cook grains and proteins separately from sauces, so lunches don't go mushy by Thursday
- Friday lunch comes out of the freezer, not the fridge. 5 days in the fridge is too long for chicken
- Double the curry and chili recipes and freeze half
- Keep a "lazy backup" in the freezer for weeks when Sunday prep doesn't happen
