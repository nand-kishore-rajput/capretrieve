# Back Pain Log

#health #backpain

Pain score is 1 to 10. 1 = don't notice it, 5 = constant ache and I'm thinking about it, 8+ = can't sit, can't sleep. Location is lower back unless noted. I log on bad days and on a few normal days so I have a baseline.

## Context

Started this log in November 2024 after the physio asked me to "keep a diary, with numbers." Mostly lower back, left side, sometimes radiating into the left glute. No numbness or tingling in the legs (the physio asked about that every time). Background: desk job, 8 hours a day plus evening study, and almost no exercise at the time.

Big change in January 2025: [[Standing Desk]]. Appointments and what the doctor and physio said are in [[Doctor Visits]].

## Patterns

- **Triggers:** long sitting without breaks (3+ hours), bad chairs, lifting things with a rounded back, skipping the home program for more than a week, poor sleep
- **What helps:** standing desk schedule, physio home program, walking at lunch, running (surprisingly), heat pad in the evening on bad days
- **What doesn't help much:** ibuprofen alone (masks it for a night), stretching only when it already hurts
- Pain seems to lag behind behavior by about 1 to 2 days. A bad Tuesday usually shows up on Wednesday or Thursday
