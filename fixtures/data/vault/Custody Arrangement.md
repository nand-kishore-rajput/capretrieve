# Custody Arrangement

Notes on how Jana and I share time with Mila (8, in 3rd grade). We kept joint legal custody (gemeinsames Sorgerecht), so this is only about where she lives day to day and how we split weeks and holidays. Money questions live in [[Divorce Settlement]], and the legal advice is logged in [[Lawyer Meetings]]. #private #family

## Where we started

After I moved out on 2024-12-01 we improvised: Mila was with Jana during the week and with me every other weekend plus Wednesday afternoons. That's basically the classic Residenzmodell. It worked for the first few months but Mila started asking on 2025-02-09 why she "only visits" my place. That hit hard.

Jugendamt counselling session on 2025-03-05 (free, ~90 minutes). The counsellor's main points:

- Kids around 8 usually cope well with more balanced models **if the parents live close and the school stays the same**.
- Whatever we pick, keep handovers predictable and do them at school when possible, so Mila isn't standing between us at a door.
- Don't ask Mila to choose. Ask her what she likes about each home instead.

## Options we considered

### 1. Residenzmodell (every other weekend)
- Pros: one main home, least disruption, simplest for Jana.
- Cons: I see Mila about 4 to 5 days a month. Too little for me, and Mila clearly wants more.
- Child support: full amount per the Düsseldorfer Tabelle, currently €558/month at my income bracket.

### 2. Alternating weeks (7/7)
- Pros: fewest handovers (one per week), real everyday life in both homes.
- Cons: 7 days without seeing the other parent is long for an 8-year-old. Mila said she'd "miss Mama too much". Also clashes with my Tuesday/Thursday lectures in the winter semester.

### 3. 2-2-3 rotation
- Mon/Tue with one parent, Wed/Thu with the other, Fri to Sun alternating.
- Pros: never more than 3 days apart from either parent.
- Cons: lots of handovers, Mila's stuff constantly in the wrong flat. We tested it for 3 weeks in April 2025 and it was chaos. Lost PE kit twice, the hamster went back and forth once (never again).

### 4. Extended weekend model (what we chose, see below)
- Every other weekend from Thursday after school to Monday morning drop-off, plus every Wednesday afternoon and overnight.
- Roughly a 64/36 split. Not a full Wechselmodell, but close to it.

## Current agreement (signed 2025-07-15)

Signed as part of the parenting plan, not yet in a court order. Dr. Vossberg says that's fine as long as we both stick to it.

**Regular schedule**
- **Week A**: Mila with me Wednesday after Hort until Thursday morning, then again Thursday after school until Monday school drop-off.
- **Week B**: Mila with me Wednesday after Hort until Thursday morning. Rest of the week with Jana.
- Handovers happen at school or Hort wherever possible. If not, at the playground on Arthur-Hoffmann-Straße, never inside either flat.

**Holidays (Saxony school calendar)**
- Summer (6 weeks): 3 weeks each, split into two blocks so neither of us has her for more than 2 weeks in a row. 2025: I had 2025-07-07 to 2025-07-20 and 2025-08-04 to 2025-08-10.
- Autumn break: alternates yearly. 2025 is mine (2025-10-06 to 2025-10-18). We're going to my parents in Freiburg for the first week.
- Christmas: Christmas Eve and the 25th with Jana in odd years, with me in even years. The 26th to New Year swaps accordingly. So **2025: Christmas Eve with Jana**, I get 2025-12-26 to 2026-01-01.
- Winter break (February): split in half.
- Easter: whoever doesn't have Christmas Eve that year gets Easter Sunday.
- Birthdays: Mila's birthday (2025-05-11) we both attend whatever party there is. Each parent gets their own birthday with her if it falls on a school day evening.

**Other rules we agreed on**
- Video call allowed every evening at 19:00 with the parent who doesn't have her, max 15 minutes, Mila can skip if she wants.
- Either parent may travel abroad with her for up to 14 days with 4 weeks' notice to the other.
- Doctor appointments: whoever has her that day goes. Both have the Kinderarzt details and a copy of the health insurance card.
- Screen time: same rules in both homes, 45 minutes on school days.
