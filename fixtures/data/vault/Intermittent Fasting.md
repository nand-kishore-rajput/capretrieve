# Intermittent Fasting

#health #nutrition

## The experiment

16:8 fasting: eat within an 8-hour window, fast for the other 16. My window was 12:00 to 20:00. I ran it for 8 weeks, from 2025-04-07 to 2025-06-01.

Why I tried it:
- I'd been snacking constantly in the evenings during thesis-writing sessions, mostly bread, cheese and chocolate after 22:00
- Weight had crept from 81 kg to 86.5 kg over about 18 months
- Two colleagues swore by it for focus in the mornings
- It looked simpler than counting calories, since there's only one rule

Rules I set:
- Black coffee, plain tea, water and sparkling water allowed during the fast
- No milk in coffee during the fast (this was the hardest rule)
- First meal at 12:00, last bite by 20:00
- No calorie counting. Same type of food as usual, mostly from [[Meal Prep]]
- One flexible day per week allowed for social stuff

Baseline before starting (2025-04-06):
- Weight 86.5 kg
- Waist 96 cm
- Resting heart rate 64 bpm (watch)
- Energy, self-rated 1 to 10: around 5 most days

End result after 8 weeks:
- Weight 83.8 kg (down 2.7 kg)
- Waist 93 cm (down 3 cm)
- Resting HR 61 bpm
- Average energy weeks 5 to 8: about 6.2, compared with 5 before

## What worked

- **Killing the late-night snacking.** The real win. A hard 20:00 cutoff removed 400 to 600 kcal of chocolate and bread a night. I suspect most of the weight loss came from this, not from anything about fasting itself
- **Morning focus.** From week 3, the 09:00 to 11:30 block was my most productive time of day. No post-breakfast slump
- **Simplicity.** One rule is easy to follow even in a chaotic week
- **Pairing with meal prep.** Having lunch boxes ready at 12:00 stopped me from breaking the fast with a bakery pastry
- **Sparkling water at 10:30.** Weirdly effective against the late-morning hunger wave

## What didn't work

- **Fasted running.** Anything over 5 km before noon was miserable. I moved long runs to the afternoon
- **Social dinners.** Friends eat at 20:00 or later. I either broke the window or sat there with a water. Not sustainable long term
- **Overeating at 12:00.** First two weeks I'd demolish a huge lunch plus a snack and feel sluggish by 14:00. Fixed by eating a normal-sized lunch and a planned snack at 16:00
- **Black coffee only.** I hated it. By week 6 I was cheating with a splash of oat milk, maybe 20 kcal, and decided I don't care
- **Office days.** The canteen closes at 13:30 and team lunches happen at 11:45. I usually moved the window earlier, 11:30 to 19:30

## After the experiment (June to September)

Went to a looser version: 14:10 most weekdays (10:00 to 20:00), with no rules on weekends. Weight stayed between 83.5 and 84.5 kg all summer. The 20:00 cutoff is the part that stuck.

## Restart after blood test (October 2025)

The October bloods came back with HbA1c at 7.2%, which is well above where it should be. Details in [[Blood Test Results]]. The doctor didn't tell me to fast, but she said reducing evening eating and losing a few more kilos would help, and that time-restricted eating is fine as long as I don't compensate with huge meals.

New plan from 2025-10-20:
- Back to 16:8 on weekdays, window 11:30 to 19:30
- Weekends 14:10
- Lunch built around protein and vegetables, fewer refined carbs. Swapping the white rice in my meal prep for lentils or bulgur
- No sugary drinks at all, including the "healthy" juice I'd been buying
- 20-minute walk after dinner, every day
- Track energy again with the same 1 to 10 scale
- Re-check with the doctor before the repeat HbA1c in about 3 months
