# Running Plan

#running #health

## Goal

Run a 10k in under 60 minutes at the city summer run on **2025-07-13**. Stretch goal: under 57 minutes.

Starting point (April 2025): hadn't run regularly since university. First test on 2025-04-12 was 3 km in 21:30 with two walking breaks, heart rate maxed around 182. My back had been good enough for a few weeks to try.

Why running: the physio said low-intensity cardio would help the back, and it's cheap (shoes 120 EUR).

## The plan (12 weeks, 3 to 4 runs a week)

Paces are rough. "Easy" means I can talk in full sentences, about 6:45 to 7:15 per km at the start.

Rules:
- Never increase weekly distance by more than about 10 to 15 percent
- If the back hits 4/10 or more, the next run becomes a walk
- Long runs in the afternoon, never fasted (learned that the hard way, see week 3)
- Runs finish before 19:00 so they don't wreck sleep
- 10 minutes of the physio routine after every run

## Sleep and running

The connection is obvious in the data from [[Sleep Tracking]]. Weeks where I average under 7 hours, my easy-run heart rate is about 6 to 8 bpm higher at the same pace, and the long run feels much harder. Week 5 of the plan was the clearest example. On days I run before 19:00, I fall asleep faster.
