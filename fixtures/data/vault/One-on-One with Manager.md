# One-on-One with Manager

Running log of my 1:1s with Markus Brenner (Engineering Manager, Billing). Biweekly, Thursdays 14:00, 30 minutes, his office or video. He became my manager in January 2025 after my previous manager moved to the Berlin office. I started writing these down in more detail around May, when the conversations started to feel different. #career #1on1

Newest entries at the bottom.

- He wants "more visible ownership" from senior engineers. Fine.
- I mentioned my master's program (part-time, two evenings a week, plus exams in February and July). He said "no problem as long as delivery doesn't suffer".

## 2025-02-13

- I said I disagreed and asked for everything in writing. Nina said I'd get a document next week.
- Next steps are in [[Performance Improvement Plan]].

## Patterns I see looking back

- **Expectations moved.** In February: coordinate cross-team. In June: "you're paid to build". In September: "hard to work with in planning", i.e. the coordination he asked for.
- **No written goals.** I asked three times (July, August, September). Never got them before the PIP talk.
- **The master's.** Brought up twice as a capacity issue, never with evidence.
- **Anonymous feedback.** No names, no examples.
- **Timing.** The tone changed right after the Sev-2 and got sharper after my DD estimate disagreed with leadership.
- **Positive signals from above Markus** (Claudia's comments on KR 2.1, the DD assignment) don't seem to reach him, or don't matter to him.

## What I want to do differently

- Send a short written summary to Markus after every 1:1 from now on, same day, by email. "Here's what I understood, let me know if anything is wrong."
- Keep copies of all of it in a private folder, not only on the company laptop.
- Stop over-explaining in the meetings. Say less, write more.
- Talk to someone outside work about this. Maybe the works council (Betriebsrat), maybe a lawyer. Need to find out what my rights are here after 6 years.
