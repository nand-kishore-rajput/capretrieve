# Severance Negotiation

The company wants me gone. Six years in, the [[Performance Improvement Plan]] was clearly the first step toward an exit, and HR has now hinted at an Aufhebungsvertrag. Notes on how severance works in Germany and how I want to play this. The financial side is modelled in [[Monthly Budget]]. #private #career

## Facts about my situation

- Start date: **2019-09-01**, so 6 years of service as of September 2025.
- Gross salary: **€6,200/month**, €74,400/year.
- Company has ~400 employees in Germany, so the Kündigungsschutzgesetz definitely applies (threshold is more than 10 employees, and I'm well past the 6-month waiting period).
- Notice period per contract and § 622 BGB: with 5+ years of service, **2 months to the end of a calendar month**.
- PIP started 2025-08-04, runs 90 days, so ends around 2025-11-02.
- No written warnings (Abmahnungen) in my file. Checked my personnel file on 2025-10-01, which you're allowed to request.

## What's typical in Germany

What I've pieced together from reading and from talking to Herr Lindqvist (employment lawyer, first consultation on 2025-10-16):

### Kündigungsschutzgesetz (KSchG)
- Once the KSchG applies, the employer needs a valid reason to dismiss: conduct-related, personal (incl. performance), or operational.
- **Performance-based dismissal is hard.** The employer has to prove I'm significantly underperforming compared to comparable colleagues (the courts talk about roughly a third below average) over a longer period, and that I was warned. A PIP alone isn't enough.
- If they dismiss me anyway, I have **3 weeks** from receiving the notice to file a Kündigungsschutzklage. Miss that and the dismissal is valid no matter how bad it was. **Calendar reminder is set for that the day any letter arrives.**

### Rule of thumb for severance
- There's no general legal right to severance. It's negotiated.
- The common starting formula: **0.5 gross monthly salaries per year of service**.
- For me: 0.5 × €6,200 × 6 = **€18,600**.
- The factor goes up or down depending on how weak the employer's case is. A shaky performance case plus no Abmahnung means factors of 0.75 to 1.0, sometimes more, are realistic according to Herr Lindqvist.
  - Factor 0.75: €27,900
  - Factor 1.0: €37,200
  - Factor 1.25: €46,500

### Aufhebungsvertrag (termination agreement)
- Mutual agreement to end the contract. No dismissal, so no court involvement.
- **Big risk: Sperrzeit.** If I sign voluntarily, the Agentur für Arbeit can block unemployment benefit for up to 12 weeks and shorten the total entitlement. Avoided if:
  - the agreement states the employer would otherwise have dismissed me for operational/personal reasons on the same date,
  - the regular notice period is respected, and
  - the severance stays within 0.25 to 0.5 monthly salaries per year (they don't check the exact justification then).
  - The higher the severance, the more carefully the wording has to be done. Lindqvist will draft that part.
- Ending the contract earlier than the notice period would allow also triggers a Ruhen (pause) of ALG. **Keep the regular notice period.**
- Severance is taxable but can use the **Fünftelregelung**, which lowers the progression effect. No social security contributions on it.

## My numbers

- **Walk-away minimum: €28,000** (factor ~0.75). Below that I'd rather let them dismiss me and sue.
- **Target: €40,000.**
- **Opening ask: €52,000** plus the extras below.

### Non-cash items to ask for (cheap for them, valuable to me)
1. **Garden leave** (Freistellung) from signing until the end date, paid in full, holiday counted against it. Gives me time for job search and the thesis.
2. **Excellent reference** (Arbeitszeugnis), grade "sehr gut", draft written by me, agreed in the contract.
3. **End date 2026-03-31.** That respects the notice period and gives me salary through Q1.
4. **Accelerated vesting** of the ESPP tranche due 2026-02 (~€3,100). Also relevant to the divorce numbers.
5. Company laptop to keep (book value near zero).
6. **Sprinter clause** (Turboklausel): if I find a new job before 2026-03-31 I can leave early and get the remaining salary added to the severance.
7. Neutral wording: "termination at the employer's instigation for operational reasons". Important for the Sperrzeit.

## Strategy

### Principles
- **Sign nothing in the room.** Ever. Always "I'll have it reviewed and get back to you within a week."
- Stay professional and keep doing my job well during the PIP.
- Document everything: daily notes on PIP tasks.
- Let them make the first number.
- My leverage: no Abmahnung, positive reviews in 2021 to 2023, the PIP goals were vague and changed twice. Their weak spot is that a performance dismissal would be very hard to defend in court and they know it.
- My weak spot: I need the income stability more than they need a quick exit, mostly because of the divorce. Don't show that.

### Plan of play
1. **Wait for the PIP to end (2025-11-02).** Keep a clean record until then.
2. When HR raises the Aufhebungsvertrag: listen, take notes, ask for the offer in writing.
3. Hand it to Herr Lindqvist. He replies on my behalf, citing the weak dismissal case and asking for €52k plus extras.
4. Expect a counteroffer around €20 to 25k. Move slowly. Concede on the laptop first, then the number in steps of €3 to 4k.
5. If they refuse to go above €28k: decline, keep working, let them dismiss, file a claim within 3 weeks. Court settlements (Vergleich) usually land around 0.5 to 1.0 anyway.
