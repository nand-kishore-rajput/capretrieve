## The apartment

This is the big one. Three options on the table:

1. **Sell and split.** Net proceeds after the mortgage, the early repayment penalty (Vorfälligkeitsentschädigung, quoted at €9,800) and agent fees would be around €175,000, so roughly €87k each. Clean, but Mila would have to move.
2. **Jana buys me out.** She takes over the mortgage and pays me my share of the equity. Her bank said on 2025-08-20 she can only borrow about €60k extra, which isn't enough without help from her parents.
3. **Jana stays, I stay on the mortgage until 2029.** I'm not willing to do this. If the job goes I can't carry liability on a flat I don't live in.

My counterproposal: Jana buys me out at **€80,000**, a bit under my share in return for netting it against the €15,500 Zugewinn I owe her. So she pays me about €64,500 cash and I get released from the loan. Her parents have hinted they could put in €20k. That would close the gap.

## Pension equalisation (Versorgungsausgleich)

The court does this automatically unless we both opt out in a notarised agreement, and Dr. Vossberg advised against opting out.

- Statutory pension points earned during the marriage: me **48.6 Entgeltpunkte**, Jana **29.1** (she worked part-time from 2017 after Mila was born).
- Each side transfers half of their marriage-period points to the other, so in effect I lose about **9.75 points** net. At today's value (~€39.32 per point per month) that's about €383/month less pension for me at 67.
- My company pension (bAV via Direktversicherung) has about €18,400 in it. It gets split internally by the insurer, so Jana gets her own contract of around €9,200.
- Jana's small Riester contract (~€4,100) gets split as well.

Honestly this feels fair. She cut back her hours so that I could do the evening master's program. I'm not going to fight it.

## My priorities, in order

1. **Mila stays in her school and her routine.** If that means Jana keeps the flat and I take a smaller payout, fine.
2. **Get off the mortgage.** Non-negotiable, especially with the work situation.
3. **No ongoing spousal maintenance**, a one-off payment if I have to.
4. **Keep it out of court.** Every court hearing costs €1,500+ in fees and wrecks a week of my sleep.
5. Keep the depot mostly intact. It's my only real safety net.
