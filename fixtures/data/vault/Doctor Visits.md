### 2025-10-14: GP, results discussion
- Full results in [[Blood Test Results]]
- **HbA1c 7.2%** and fasting glucose 138 mg/dL. Both in the diabetes range. She wants to repeat both before making the diagnosis official
- LDL 162 mg/dL, triglycerides 185. High
- ALT slightly up, probably mild fatty liver
- Vitamin D low at 18 ng/mL
- Plan agreed:
  - 3 months of lifestyle changes before discussing medication: weight loss of 4 to 6 kg, fewer refined carbs, no sugary drinks, strength training twice a week, walk after meals
  - Measure blood pressure at home twice daily for 2 weeks
  - Vitamin D 1000 IU daily
  - Repeat bloods in January (HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipids, liver values)
- If diabetes is confirmed: referral to ophthalmologist, yearly foot exam, diabetes education course
- I asked about the back too. She said the [[Back Pain Log]] numbers look great and to keep the home program going, especially with strength training now on the list
- Appointment took 25 minutes, longer than any GP visit I've ever had. She was very calm about it, which helped

How I felt afterwards: shaken, honestly. Sat on a bench outside for 15 minutes before going home. Called my father in the evening. He said he wished he'd been told at 36 instead of 52.
