## Scenario: losing the job

This is the one I keep running at 2 a.m., so let's put it on paper.

**Assumptions**
- Last working day around 2026-03-31 under an Aufhebungsvertrag.
- Arbeitslosengeld I: about 60% of the previous net (no child in my household for tax purposes, so 60% rather than 67%). Based on the capped gross that's roughly **€2,250/month** for up to 12 months.
- Risk: a **Sperrzeit of up to 12 weeks** if I sign the Aufhebungsvertrag without a valid reason. That's 3 months with zero ALG. Has to be avoided or covered by the wording of the agreement (see [[Severance Negotiation]]).
- Severance: rule-of-thumb amount ~€18,600 gross, my target is higher. Taxed with the Fünftelregelung, maybe 60–65% left net.

**Monthly budget on ALG I**

| | Now | On ALG I |
|---|---|---|
| Income | 3,950 | 2,250 |
| Fixed costs | 2,588 | 2,430 (no gym, cheaper phone plan, no tutoring income either) |
| Child support | (incl. above) | might drop to a lower bracket, maybe €480 |
| Variable | 750 | 600 |
| **Balance** | **+612** | **~ −700** |

So without a job I'd burn about €700/month, more if the mortgage share is still running. With €9,850 in the emergency fund plus a net severance of, say, €15,000, I'd have **~35 months** of runway on paper. Realistically less, because the settlement payments will eat some of it.

**What I'd cut first**
1. Eating out down to €40
2. Pause the ETF plan (but not Mila's €50)
3. Move to a cheaper phone and internet plan, −€35
4. Ask the university about a fee reduction, or take a semester off. Last resort, the thesis matters to me.

**What I won't cut**
- Mila's swimming lessons and anything school-related
- Child support payments on time, always. Dr. Vossberg was very clear about that.
- The gym. My back gets worse without it.
