## Meeting log

### 2025-01-20, initial consultation (75 min)
- Explained the situation: separated since 2024-12-01, one child, jointly owned flat, no prenup.
- Advice:
  - The Trennungsjahr has to be complete before filing. Document the separation date (my rental contract for the Gohlis flat starting 2024-12-01 is good evidence).
  - Zugewinngemeinschaft applies. Gather statements for all assets **on the wedding date (2015-06-13)** and on the date the divorce application is served.
  - Don't move money out of joint accounts. Don't stop paying the mortgage, even though I've moved out. It'll be offset later.
  - Child support: start paying voluntarily right away, according to the Düsseldorfer Tabelle. Looks much better than waiting for a demand.
- Fee: €360 + VAT (Erstberatung, capped).

### 2025-02-26, documents and asset overview (60 min)
- Went through my depot statements, payslips for the last 12 months, the 2023 and 2024 tax returns, the mortgage contract.
- She flagged that about €6,000 of the depot predates the marriage, so that comes off my starting value (Anfangsvermögen). Need the 2015 statement from the bank. Requested it, costs €15.
- ESPP shares: count at market value on the cut-off date even if unvested? Her answer: vested ones yes, unvested ones it depends. She'll check.
- Fee: €240 + VAT.

### 2025-03-18, joint meeting with Jana and her lawyer (2 h)
- Both sides agreed in principle to settle out of court.
- Jana's lawyer raised spousal maintenance for the first time.
- Agreed to sort custody separately and first, so money talks don't spill into it.
- Fee: €480 + VAT. Expensive but worth it, the atmosphere was okay.

### 2025-05-07, custody options (45 min)
- Talked through Residenzmodell vs. Wechselmodell vs. something in between.
- Advice: a written parenting plan signed by both is enough for now. A court order only makes sense if things get contentious. Courts in Saxony look closely at what's actually been happening, so a stable routine is my best argument.
- With a near 50/50 split the child support would be calculated from both incomes. With 64/36 I still pay pretty much the full table amount, maybe slightly reduced.
- Fee: €180 + VAT.

### 2025-07-01, settlement draft review (90 min)
- Went through the draft from Jana's side (received 2025-06-24) line by line.
- Key points:
  - Their apartment buyout figure of €95k uses a valuation that's too high and ignores the early repayment penalty. She thinks €80 to 85k is defensible.
  - The €350/month spousal maintenance for 2 years: weak claim, Jana is back at 32 hours and has a stable job. Suggested a one-off payment as a counteroffer.
  - Versorgungsausgleich: advised strongly against waiving it. A waiver needs a notary and gets checked by the court for fairness anyway. Just let it run.
- Fee: €360 + VAT.

### 2025-09-12, Zugewinn calculation (60 min)
- She ran the numbers. My net gain is about €31,000 higher than Jana's, so the balancing payment is around €15,500.
- My mortgage payments since moving out (~€6,900 so far) can be claimed back in part. Keep every bank statement.
- Agreed on the counterproposal strategy: buyout at €80k, netted against the Zugewinn, one-off €5,000 instead of maintenance.
- Fee: €240 + VAT.

### 2025-10-09, job situation and Jana's possible move (40 min, phone)
- Told her about the PIP and that the company is likely to offer an Aufhebungsvertrag.
- Her advice:
  - **Don't sign anything at work before talking to an employment lawyer.** She's not one, but recommended a colleague, Herr Lindqvist.
  - A severance payment generally counts as income for maintenance purposes, spread over a reasonable period. It doesn't just disappear from the calculation.
  - A drop in income can reduce child support, but only if it's not self-inflicted. Voluntarily signing an Aufhebungsvertrag without a good reason could be held against me. Get the reasons documented.
  - On Jana moving: a move that takes Mila out of her school needs agreement from both parents under joint custody. Nothing to do now, but if it gets concrete, get in touch immediately.
- Fee: €160 + VAT.
